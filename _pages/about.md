Hello! I am a Ph.D. student in the Graduate School of Engineering Biology at KAIST, working in the Systems Biology and Medicine Laboratory under the supervision of Prof. Hyun Uk Kim.

My research lies at the intersection of computational biology, artificial intelligence, and natural-product biosynthesis. I develop computational and data-driven approaches to understand how microbial genomes encode, regulate, and produce specialized metabolites with potential pharmaceutical applications.

My current research interests include genome language models for gene-expression prediction, biosynthetic gene cluster analysis, natural-product pathway reconstruction, evolutionary genomics of modular biosynthetic enzymes, and molecular modeling for enzyme engineering. Ultimately, I aim to connect genomic information with biosynthetic activity and chemical structure to support the discovery and engineering of microbial natural products.

## Research Interests

* AI and machine learning for biology
* Microbial natural-product biosynthesis
* Biosynthetic gene clusters
* Genome language models and gene-expression prediction
* Systems and synthetic biology
* Molecular modeling and enzyme engineering
